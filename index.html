Elsa

20__  —  2025
Always our girl.

For the Boys

Elsa had been really sick for a while — the kind of sick where her body was hurting a lot, and even the medicine the vet gave her wasn’t helping anymore.

We took her to the vet, and they helped her fall asleep peacefully. No more pain, no more hurting. We were right there with her the whole time so she knew she wasn’t alone. It was calm and quiet, and she felt safe.

We know this is really hard. It’s okay to cry. It’s okay to be mad, or sad, or to just not know how you feel. We feel all of that too. There’s no wrong way to feel when you lose someone you love.

She was a good girl. The best. She knew every single day of her life that she was loved — because she was, and she always will be.

She’s always going to be our dog. That part doesn’t go away.
